Read-Along

Your books, read aloud with the sentence highlighted. Works offline once a book is added.

    1. Make the book on your laptop with Export for iPhone…, and put the .epub in iCloud Drive (or AirDrop it to Files).
    2. Tap Add a book and pick it in Files. It's copied into this app, so you won't need the internet after that.
    3. For airplane mode, open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and add your books from the Home Screen app. Safari and the Home Screen app keep separate copies.
